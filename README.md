# FrameDeck

Free Mac app to manage the art on your Samsung **The Frame** TVs: batch upload with or without a mat, per-TV library,
display / delete / copy, resolution and sharpness checks, automatic rotation, and free museum art (Met, Cleveland, Rijksmuseum).

**Website:** deployed with Netlify from `index.html` at the repository root.

> Tested on the author's own Frame TVs (including QN50LS03DAFXZC). Other models and firmware may behave differently.
> Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Samsung. Provided "as is", without warranty.

Built on [samsungtvws](https://github.com/xchwarze/samsung-tv-ws-api) (LGPL-3.0), Electron and Pillow.
Problems or questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/deedeetype/FrameDeck/issues) with your TV model.

**Download:** [FrameDeck-arm64.dmg](https://github.com/deedeetype/FrameDeck/releases/latest/download/FrameDeck-arm64.dmg) (Mac with Apple Silicon).
